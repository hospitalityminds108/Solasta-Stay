Explore Nearby Attractions

Located in the heart of Wakad with seamless access to the Mumbai–Pune Highway, Solasta Stays offers excellent connectivity to some of Pune’s key attractions, business hubs, and lifestyle destinations. Whether you're visiting for work or leisure, these nearby locations ensure convenience, entertainment, and easy travel during your stay

Phoenix Mall of the Millennium Wakad

One of the largest and most popular shopping destinations in the area, Phoenix Mall of the Millennium offers a complete lifestyle experience with premium retail stores, dining outlets, entertainment zones, and multiplex cinemas. Ideal for shopping, casual outings, and family time, it is a must-visit destination located just a short drive from the property.

Hinjawadi IT Park

A major IT and business hub of Pune, Hinjewadi IT Park is home to numerous multinational companies, tech parks, and corporate offices. Its close proximity makes Solasta Stays an ideal choice for business travellers, offering easy access for meetings, work visits, and corporate stays without long commute times

Balewadi High Street

A vibrant lifestyle and dining destination, Balewadi High Street is known for its trendy restaurants, cafes, pubs, and boutique stores. Perfect for evening outings, casual meetings, or weekend relaxation, it offers a lively atmosphere with a wide range of food and entertainment options.

Talegaon MIDC

An important industrial zone near Pune, Talegaon MIDC houses several manufacturing units, warehouses, and corporate facilities. Its connectivity from Wakad makes Solasta Stays a convenient accommodation option for professionals visiting for industrial work, site visits, or business operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in starts from 2:00 PM, and check-out is until 11:00 AM.

2. Do you have parking available?

Yes, we provide parking facilities for our guests.

3. Is the banquet hall suitable for corporate events?

Yes, our banquet venues are ideal for corporate meetings, conferences, training sessions, annual meets, and social events.

4. Do you offer packages for social events?

Yes, we offer customized packages for weddings and social celebrations to take care of décor, catering, seating arrangements, and overall event coordination — ensuring a seamless and stress-free experience.
